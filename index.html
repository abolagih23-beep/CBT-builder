Accepted formats

Paste it however you have it. These all work:

  • Numbering like 1. 1) Q1: Question 1, or no numbers with a blank line between questions.
  • Options as A. A) (A) a. or all on one line.
  • Answer as "Answer: B", "Ans B", "Correct answer: Chloroplast", or a mark on the right option (*, a tick, bold, or "(correct)").
  • All the answers after the last question: "Answers: 1-B 2-C 3-A", or one per line (1. B, Q1: B, 1) (B)), optionally with the option text or an explanation after each.
  • Rows pasted from Excel or Google Sheets, or CSV: question, options, answer, explanation (a header row is optional).
  • JSON with question, options and answer fields.
Theory format 
1. Explain how photosynthesis supports life. [6 marks]
Answer: optional model answer or marking guide

2. Describe the water cycle.
Marks: 5
Answer: optional model answer

Number each question, or leave a blank line between unnumbered questions. Marks can go in brackets after the question or on a "Marks:" line. "Answer:", "Model answer:" or "Marking guide:" is optional. Put a blank line before each new numbered question that follows an answer.
